How orbs and tickets work

Packs cost 20 orbs each or one matching ticket. Start with 100 orbs; earn 10 per achievement, 25 per milestone and 1 per 25 Elo above your previous high. One Classic ticket is added every 24 hours; choose a colored ticket for each milestone. Tickets are saved on this device; shards and orbs are saved to your account. Tickets and draw results are client-controlled, not tamper-proof. Milestones limit rarities.
